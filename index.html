Step-By-Step Blueprint

Your 4-Stage Launch Process

Follow this linear checklist so you don't waste time on busywork.

1
Day 1

Define an Urgent Problem & Micro-Niche

Avoid "selling AI to everyone". Target a specific persona (e.g., fitness coaches who need social clips, or realtors who need fast listings).

2
Day 2

Package the Minimum Viable Offer

Set up a one-page portfolio or Gumroad listing. Define exactly what the client receives, delivery timeline, and price tag ($199–$499).

3
Day 3–5

Automate the Delivery Pipeline

Create your prompt library and Make.com workflows. Run a test order for yourself to ensure you can deliver the service within 60 minutes.

4
Day 6+

Execute Cold Outreach & Social Proof

Send 15 personalized free audit samples each day. Offer your first 2 clients a 50% discount in exchange for a video testimonial.
